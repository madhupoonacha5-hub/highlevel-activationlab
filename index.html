      Experiment rules and data boundaries
      Synthetic enrollment uses one subscribing account per randomization unit. The two plays are mutually exclusive. The control receives the existing experience. Outcomes include all assigned accounts, including unreached treatment accounts. Each play has a 14-day outcome window and a Day 90 decision. The demo uses an approximate 95% interval for the difference in proportions and a two-sided 1% assignment check for the planned 50/50 split. Scale requires the lower bound to clear the minimum lift and opt-outs to stay within +1 percentage point. A failed assignment check blocks the decision.
      Product events, Trial Experience, subscription, Implementation, support AI and billing are simulated feeds. The outreach adapter writes decisions to the audit ledger. It does not send email or SMS. Proposed intake placement, data contracts and funnel definitions would be validated with HighLevel.
